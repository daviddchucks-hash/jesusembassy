RCCG Baptismal Class

Ongoing · Jesus Embassy Kaduna

Baptism is a meaningful public expression of faith in Jesus Christ. It points to repentance, new life, and our union with Christ in His death and resurrection.

Jesus Embassy Kaduna invites believers who are ready for this next step to take part in the baptismal class. The class creates space to learn, ask questions, and prepare thoughtfully for baptism within the life of the church. Contact the church office for the next class date and registration details.
