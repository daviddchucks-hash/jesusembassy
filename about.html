Who We Are

About Jesus Embassy Kaduna

Our Story

How We Began

Jesus Embassy Kaduna is a local parish of the Redeemed Christian Church of God, serving the city through worship, prayer, teaching, and practical care. Our story is still being written in the lives of people who encounter Jesus, find belonging, and discover a place to serve.

Our Vision

A worshipping family in every community

We want every person and family we meet to encounter the love of God, grow in holiness, and become a faithful witness for Jesus Christ.

Our Mission

Make disciples, serve Kaduna, reach the nations

Following the heart of RCCG's mission, we help people follow Jesus, nurture believers through God's Word, and carry the good news into our homes, city, and world.

Our Foundation

What We Believe

Our faith is anchored in the Holy Scriptures and in the good news of Jesus Christ. These concise statements are adapted from the public doctrinal teaching of the Redeemed Christian Church of God.

The Scriptures

We receive the Old and New Testaments as inspired by the Holy Spirit and as the trustworthy foundation for Christian faith and life.

The Trinity

There is one God, revealed as Father, Son, and Holy Spirit — three Persons, one God, worthy of worship and obedience.

Salvation

Jesus Christ is the Son of God and Saviour of the world. Through His death and resurrection, He offers forgiveness, new life, and reconciliation with God.

The Holy Spirit

The Holy Spirit teaches, sanctifies, empowers, and equips believers to live faithfully and bear witness to Christ.

The Church

The Church is a community of believers called to worship God, proclaim the gospel, care for people, and grow together in holiness.

The Second Coming

We look forward to the return of Jesus Christ and live with hope, readiness, and the call to share His gospel with every nation.
