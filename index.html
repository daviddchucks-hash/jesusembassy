Welcome

Welcome to Jesus Embassy

Whether you are returning to church or joining us for the first time, you have a place here. We gather around the gospel of Jesus Christ, make room for prayer and honest fellowship, and encourage one another to live out our faith beyond Sunday.

Service Times

Make room for worship, teaching, prayer, and fellowship throughout the week.

Sunday Service Every Sunday 8:00AM–11:30AM Sunday School followed by family worship, prayer, and the teaching of God's Word.
Midweek Service Tuesdays & Thursdays 5:30PM Go deeper in Scripture on Tuesdays and bring your questions, faith, and needs to Faith Clinic on Thursdays.
Prayer Meeting Every Saturday 7:00PM A focused time of intercession and seeking God together.
